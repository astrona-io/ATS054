# Encrypting Traffic in Transit with Cilium

This section covers encrypting Traffic in Transit with Cilium. It develops transparent Encryption And Its Traffic Boundaries through mechanisms, examples, prerequisites and failure reasoning.

Read the module goals, teaching and closing summary in order. YAML checks are indexed separately and appear after designated summaries. Larger domains include an intermediate checkpoint before the final review.

- Compare Cilium IPsec and WireGuard at a conceptual level.
